## Design Context

### Users
CMU students, professors, and TAs reviewing math homework. The HackCMU demo explores immediate learning feedback and faster grading with explicit instructor approval.

### Brand personality
Quiet, clear, familiar. Verity is a temporary project name.

### Aesthetic direction
Ivan supplied four Gradescope screenshots and requested a very similar, minimalist interface. White workspace, pale-gray navigation and review panel, dark teal actions, blue links, thin borders, restrained corners. Lato approximates the humanist sans in the reference; the actual Gradescope font could not be inspected and is not verified. System fallbacks keep the interface usable offline.

### Design principles
- Preserve the large document canvas and narrow feedback panel.
- Keep status and grading decisions legible without decorative dashboards.
- Clearly distinguish fixed sample feedback, suggested scores, and instructor-approved grades.
- Keep personal submissions out of demo fixtures.
- Reuse existing sample API behavior and keep the debug console available.

### Tokens and structure
White #ffffff; sidebar #f8f9f9; panel #f1f3f4; text #25292b; teal #1a505a; link blue #076bad. Lato body and headings, 12/14/16/20/28px equivalent rem scale. Left-aligned navigation and metadata. Dashboard: 244px sidebar and course grid. Review: 64px rail, flexible paper canvas, 368px feedback panel, bottom action bar. On mobile, feedback follows the document vertically.
