## Design Context

### Users
Professors and TAs set grading standards and review math homework. Students will later map their own submission pages to question IDs. The current pass centers Homework 1 and its teacher-facing setup.

### Brand personality
Quiet, clear, familiar. Verity is a temporary project name.

### Aesthetic direction
Ivan revised the brief: preserve the minimalist feel, not an exact Gradescope replica. White workspace, pale-gray navigation and review panel, dark teal actions, thin borders, restrained corners, and Lato typography. Start at Homeworks, not courses; Homework 1 has Graded homeworks and Grading standards tabs.

### Design principles
- Use teacher reference uploads and question configuration to define good work, then a document canvas and narrow review panel to judge submissions.
- Keep blank-assignment pages, instructor-solution pages, and student-submission pages independent. Specify expected answer structure per question.
- Keep status and grading decisions legible without decorative dashboards.
- Clearly distinguish fixed sample feedback, suggested scores, and instructor-approved grades.
- Keep personal submissions out of demo fixtures.
- Clearly label the scripted example. Uploaded PDFs and custom requirements are not automatically analyzed. The prior API debug console remains available.

### Tokens and structure
White #ffffff; sidebar #f8f9f9; panel #f1f3f4; text #25292b; teal #1a505a; link blue #076bad. Lato body and headings, 12/14/16/20/28px equivalent rem scale. Left-aligned navigation and metadata. Dashboard: 244px sidebar and course grid. Review: 64px rail, flexible paper canvas, 368px feedback panel, bottom action bar. On mobile, feedback follows the document vertically.
